Shipping

Standard shipping is $5.95 — free on subtotals of $50 or more. Orders pack and ship in 1–2 business days; delivery usually takes 4–7 business days after that, continental US only.

Unopened products: return within 30 days of delivery with your receipt for a refund. Damaged on arrival? Email a photo within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or refund — your choice.
