Good questions

Before you call

How much does a new roof cost?

Most Denver-metro replacements land between $6,900 and $14,500 depending on size, pitch and material. Your written quote is exact — the price we quote is the price you pay.

Will insurance cover my storm damage?

Often, yes. We document everything with photos, meet your adjuster on the roof, and make sure nothing is missed. You only pay your deductible if the claim is approved.

How long does a replacement take?

Most homes are finished in 1–2 days, including tear-off and cleanup. Weather delays happen in Colorado — we'll always tell you the day before, not the morning of.

What warranty do I get?

Two written warranties: a new roof carries our 25-year workmanship warranty plus the manufacturer's material warranty (25 years to lifetime depending on shingle); repairs carry a 5-year leak warranty. Both are in writing and transferable if you sell.
