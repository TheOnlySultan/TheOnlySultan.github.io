Yes. Diagnosis is a flat $59, waived in full when you approve the repair. After diagnosing, your tech shows you one exact price for the full job — approve it and we start; decline it and the $59 diagnosis fee is all you pay.

Book before noon and we'll offer same-day slots. Emergencies (no heat under 40°, active leaks, sewage) jump the queue 24/7.

Every tech is licensed in Ohio, background-checked, drug-tested and drives a stocked PolarFlow truck. License #PF-8814, fully insured.

Parts and labor on the repair we performed. If the same issue returns within two years, the fix is free.
