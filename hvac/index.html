How it works

From first message to a lead the owner can act on

01

Chat, don't fill forms

The visitor describes the problem in their own words. The agent identifies the service and asks whether it's urgent — no heat, no cooling, or a water leak is flagged urgent automatically.

02

Questions answered on the spot

Hours, service area, and demo pricing ranges are answered mid-chat from a fixed knowledge base, without losing the visitor's place. Anything outside it is passed to the owner — never guessed.

03

One clean summary

The visitor confirms a summary card: service, urgency, name, phone, preferred callback time, and a timestamp. In a live setup, that summary goes straight to the owner's inbox.

Safety comes before sales

If a visitor mentions a gas smell, carbon monoxide, or sparking, the agent stops the booking flow immediately and tells them to leave the home and call 911 / their gas utility. Try typing in the chat to see it.

FAQ

About this demo

Is Copperline a real company?

No. Copperline Heating & Air is a fictional company created for this demo. The phone number is a 555 demo number, and no real bookings are taken.

Are the prices real quotes?

No. Every price on this page and in the chat is a clearly-labelled demo range from a fixed sample knowledge base. The agent will never invent a price that isn't in that data, and an exact price in a live setup would only be set after a real diagnosis.

Does the agent answer phone calls?

No. This is a website chat demo only — there is no phone answering. The widget is labelled that way on purpose, so a client testing it sees exactly what the product does and doesn't do.

What happens to a lead in a live setup?

The visitor confirms a summary card in chat. In a live setup, that summary goes straight to the owner's inbox (this demo note is shown in the chat itself). In this static demo, nothing leaves your browser.

What can I test in the chat?

Book a repair start-to-finish; ask about prices, hours, or the service area mid-flow; say “no heat” to see the urgent flag; type an invalid phone number; ask “are you human?”; or mention a gas smell to see the safety stop.
